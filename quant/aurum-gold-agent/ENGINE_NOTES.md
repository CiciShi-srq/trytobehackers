# AurumEngine v0.2.0 APIs

`makeDemo(days=260, seed=42)` returns `{prices,news,provenance}`. Prices has nonenumerable `.provenance`; all synthetic fixtures explicitly labelled. Demo starts 2025-11-03, weekends removed only, not an exchange calendar.

`parsePrices(csv, provenance={source,synthetic,priceBasis,volumeUnit})` returns sorted bars `{date,symbol,open,high,low,close,volume,amount}`; amount optional. Supports English field names and Chinese 日期/证券代码/开盘/最高/最低/收盘/成交量/成交额. Rejects malformed dates, duplicate date/symbol, nonpositive numbers and inconsistent OHLC. Retains original volume units; missing amount makes Amihud use close*volume approximation.

`parseNews(csv)` returns `{published_at,date,title,source,sentiment,scoring}[]`. Headers published_at,title,source,sentiment (or Chinese). Title dedup is exact after removing whitespace and lowercase. Optional sentiment must lie [-1,1]; absent score uses basic signed lexicon. Any publication dated D becomes eligible no earlier than D+1 calendar day and is allocated to first bar on/after that date. This is conservative date-only availability; time zones are not normalized.

`analyze(prices,news=[],options={factor:'mom20',factors:['mom20','sentiment'],feeBps:3,slippageBps:5})` requires >=65 bars for one symbol. `options.factors` overrides `.factor`. Returns bars/provenance/factors/newsDaily/selectedFactor/selectedFactors/split/backtest/chan/warnings. Each factor has key,label,values,trainIC,testIC,trainPearson,testPearson,direction,samples,testSamples. IC is time-series daily overlapping labels, NOT cross-sectional alpha IC; fewer than 3 eligible pairs or zero rank variance yields null. 60% chronological train and 40% test; direction and standardization fit train only. Label[t] = close[t+2]/close[t+1]-1. This leaves one close interval between computation and reference execution.

`backtest` contains equity rows `{date,nav,equity,benchmark,drawdown,position,score,return}`; ledger rows `{date,signalDate,side,price,notional,cost,targetWeight,researchOnly}`; metrics `{totalReturn,annualReturn,volatility,sharpe,maxDrawdown,intervals,tradeCount,totalCost,turnover}`; benchmark metrics. t-1 signal executes at t close reference, then earns t->t+1 close return. Only long/cash, fractional research units, no broker orders. Both benchmark and strategy terminal liquidation costs included. No cash interest, lot constraints, liquidity, trading bans or actual intraday execution. One-year sample, repeated feature selection and dictionary sensitivity do not prove predictive power.

`analyze(prices, news, {factor:'auto'})` enables deterministic factor discovery using **only training Spearman IC**. Candidates require finite trainIC and at least 30 valid training labels, sorted descending by absolute training IC with lexicographic key tie-break. Picks up to three, excluding simultaneous `mom5`/`reversal5` (exact inverses). Missing-news factors are ineligible due to insufficient samples or undefined IC. If no candidate qualifies, throws a clear error rather than fabricate signals. Explicit `options.factors` overrides auto mode. Returned `selectedFactors` is the frozen combination and `selectionAudit` records every factor as `{key,label,trainIC,trainSamples,eligible,selected,rank,reason}`; rank is null when ineligible. Reasons are `selected_by_train_abs_ic`, `inverse_duplicate`, `outside_top3`, `insufficient_train_samples`, or `undefined_train_ic`. Manual mode returns empty selectionAudit. Test IC and test returns never participate in automatic selection. This is an exploratory fixed-family selector, not formula generation, a significance test, or evidence of market alpha.

`chan(bars)` returns `{merged,fractals,strokes,centers,divergences,macd,rule}`. 3-merged-bar fractals additionally wait for a successor of the right candle, because the right candle might be expanded by containment. Events carry confirmedAt/confirmedIndex. Strokes alternate and require endpoint mergedIndex distance >=4 (five inclusive merged bars). Last endpoint can be extended/replaced by a later same-type extreme: current chart is a retrospective structure; never use the current full-series chart as historical trade input. Centers overlap last three strokes. Divergence is heuristic price extension plus smaller absolute MACD histogram area across same-direction strokes. It is not a proof of Chan theory, does not generate certified 1/2/3 buy/sell points, and is not used by the factor backtest.

`metrics`, `csvRows`, `newsFeatures`, `ranks`, `corr` also exported. Both `globalThis.AurumEngine` and CommonJS `module.exports` are provided; no dependencies/network/credentials.

News URL is retained and restricted to HTTP(S). Both sentiment and newsAttention features are null on unobserved news days; attention is conditional on observed article dates, not a claim of complete daily coverage.
